01 / Story • About VELORE

The Story Behind the Spaces

“Design should feel effortless, personal, and timeless.”

Founded twelve years ago in Malappuram, Kerala, VELORE approaches every residence as quiet architecture. Rather than ornamenting rooms, we edit volume, light, and shadow until only what feels natural remains.

Our studio takes on a limited number of private residential and boutique hospitality commissions across Kerala and South India each year so every detail — from structural layout to brushed brass pulls — is personally directed from concept to handover.

Elena Voss • Founder

Architectural Methodology

Six Quiet Phases

From initial acoustic laser surveys to white-glove handover, our deliberate six-stage methodology guarantees uncompromised precision, tranquility, and spatial permanence.

01 Phase 01 • Discovery

Discovery & Site Study

Full 3D laser survey, seasonal daylight mapping, acoustic analysis, and an unhurried consultation exploring your daily rituals and spatial aspirations.

Spatial Narrative & Palettes

Spatial orientation, tonal restraint, and curated physical sample chests containing raw quarry travertine, aged oak flitches, and tactile bouclé swatches.

Key Deliverable Material Sample Chest & Schematics
03 Phase 03 • Detailing

Technical & Millwork Design

Precision 1:20 bespoke joinery drawings, acoustic wall assemblies, recessed shadow-gap reveals, and concealed 2400K architectural lighting integration.

Direct quarry slab hand-selection, bookmatched stone veining, continuous flitch grain matching across veneers, and master bench-craft in European workshops.

On-Site Architectural Stewardship

Weekly architect oversight aligning zero-tolerance miter joints, stone grain transitions, and acoustic sealing with uncompromising discipline.
